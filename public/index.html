Job Application Mailer nothing is sent until you press Send

Your queue

One row per application. Only a valid email address is required — a row with no company gets your without company name email. Each row can pick its own email, cover letter and resume, or leave Default. Tick the rows you want — or the box at the top for all of them — and press Send; they turn into sent. Hold rows can't be ticked.
